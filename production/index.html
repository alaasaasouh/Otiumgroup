Otium Productions is an international production company creating documentaries, web series, podcasts, digital content and original productions for audiences across markets and platforms.

From the first idea to the final frame, we bring together creative vision, editorial intelligence and hands-on production expertise.

What we produce

Every story.
Its own format.

Documentary+

Factual storytelling with cinematic ambition. Character-driven films and cultural, social, business and human-interest documentaries.

Web Series+

Distinctive original episodic formats built for contemporary digital audiences.

Podcasts+

Interview-led formats, documentary podcasts and original audio series. Conversations and ideas made to be heard.

Digital Content+

Short-form, branded, editorial and social content, created with the same care as our larger productions.

Original Productions+

Our own ideas, developed alongside creators, brands, broadcasters, platforms and organisations.
